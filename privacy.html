Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the FruitSnap mobile application (the "App").

1) Who we are

FruitSnap is a casual fruit-slicing game. Contact: magnetoryus@gmail.com.

2) Data we process

FruitSnap does not require an account and does not run its own user database. Processing is limited to what is needed to run the App and related services:

  • On-device settings: preferences such as sound, haptics, and a local "ads removed" flag stored on your device.
  • Gameplay state: score and best score stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising data (third parties): identifiers and signals used by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads in the free experience.
  • Purchase data (third parties): Apple processes in-app purchase and entitlement information for the optional Remove Ads product.
  • Support email: information you choose to send us when contacting support.

3) How we use data

  • To provide gameplay and remember your settings.
  • To show ads unless you purchase Remove Ads.
  • To process and restore purchases through Apple.
  • To respond to support requests.

4) Advertising

The free version may show interstitial ads via Google AdMob. Ad providers may process device and advertising identifiers according to their own policies. If you buy Remove Ads, the App stops requesting ads.

Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5) In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store billing.

6) Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited data may be processed by service providers (Google AdMob, Apple) solely to operate ads, purchases, and the App Store.

7) Retention

Local settings remain on your device until you change them or uninstall the App. Third parties retain data under their own policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you.

8) Children's privacy

FruitSnap is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent.

9) Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. For App Store and AdMob data, you may also use controls offered by Apple and Google. Contact us at magnetoryus@gmail.com for privacy requests related to FruitSnap.

10) Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11) Contact
